# Planban v2.0.0

Planban v2.0.0 adds Online Boards: take a board online to share it, open it in any browser or on your phone, and connect ChatGPT and Claude to it. Boards on your computer stay exactly as they are, free and with no account.

## Local stays the default

- Planban still runs on your computer with no account, and your boards stay there unless you take one online. Nothing about a local board is sent anywhere.
- Taking a board online is a choice per board, and you can bring it back to your computer at any time with its full history.

## Online Boards

- **Take a board online** from Board settings, Take online, or ask your agent. The whole board moves to your Planban account, Specs, Plans and history included, and your computer keeps a backup copy. Planban stays an index, not file storage: a board goes online once it holds only text, and Planban lists anything to tidy first.
- **Invite people** as editors or viewers, by email or with a link. Editors can change cards, Specs and Plans, restore from history and mark work complete. Viewers can read everything and change nothing. Only the owner invites people, changes roles and takes the board online or back.
- **Each person chooses what their agents may do**: read, plan, or plan and complete. An agent can never do more than its person.
- **History follows the board.** Every change records who made it, and whether it was them or their agent.
- **Editing at the same time.** Edits clash only over the same field of the same card, changed since it was read; moves never clash. An agent's clashing edit is kept for it to reconcile, never lost. A Spec or Plan can be changed one section at a time. A quiet sign shows when someone's agent is working on a card.

## Goals

- **Each board can have a Goals page:** the project's goals, most important first, each one line with optional notes, plus one Context section for everything else. Retired goals stay on the page and can be restored.
- Goals show as one quiet line above the columns (folded to "Goals (n)" if you prefer), on a phone as one row.
- **Only people change goals.** Agents weigh work against them when suggesting what's next, never refuse work outside them, and never reorder the board. If your choices keep drifting from the goals, or a goal looks out of date, an agent mentions it in chat; with your go-ahead it leaves a suggested change on the Goals page for you to accept or dismiss.

## Several accounts on one computer

- Sign in several Planban accounts at once, as with the GitHub CLI: for example one for work and one personal. Each online board uses its own account on your computer automatically, so agents in each project just work.
- `planban online accounts` lists them and which boards use each; `planban online sign-in` adds one; `planban online sign-out --account` signs one out; `planban online switch` changes which account new boards use.

## Planban in your browser and on your phone

- Every online board has its own address at app.planban.ai, where you and the people you invite can read and edit it in any browser, with nothing to install. Log in with GitHub or an email code, sent by Planban.
- **A welcome that starts where you are:** invitations waiting for you come first. If you came from Planban on your computer, it helps you bring your boards online and shows them as they arrive. Otherwise it asks one question.
- **Your boards** lists your boards and the ones shared with you, with how many cards need you. Invitations waiting for you appear at the top. You can also start a new, empty board there.
- **On a phone** the board is one scrolling list grouped by column. A card opens full screen; Status and Move replace dragging, Save stays above the keyboard, and dialogs open as sheets.
- **Changes appear live**: when someone else or an agent changes the board, you see it without reloading.
- **If someone saves a Spec or Plan while you're editing it**, your text is kept and Planban shows you their version before you save again.
- **Open on the web**: online boards in the Planban app on your computer have an Open on the web link in the board menu and Board settings.
- Dropdowns across the board and the online screens have been redesigned: as wide as their value, with the arrow beside the text, and the phone's own picker on a phone.
- **A one-line board header**, on the web and on your computer: the Planban card stack takes you to all your boards, then the board's name opens the board switcher. On your computer, Update appears beside the board's tools when there's an update.

## Agents and AI apps

- **Codex and Claude Code** with the Planban plugin work with online boards once your computer is signed in, through the same tools as local boards.
- **ChatGPT and claude.ai** connect to `https://app.planban.ai/mcp` as a custom connector: sign in and click Allow. Their search results link straight to the card on the web page.
- **Sign in on any device.** A computer or agent without a browser of its own (a remote, cloud or headless machine) can run `planban online sign-in https://app.planban.ai --no-browser`: Planban shows a short code and a link, you open the link on any device where you're signed in, your phone included, check the code and tap Allow.
- **Access keys** for agents that can't sign in themselves can be created on the web page, in Connections: one board, what it may do and when it expires. The key is shown once.
- **Connections** on the web page and in the app lists every computer, browser, app and key signed in to your account, with when each was last used, and lets you cut any of them off, or all of them at once.

## Privacy and security

- A browser stays signed in for at most 7 days. Signing out, cutting a browser off in Connections, or Cut off everything ends it at once, and it needs a fresh sign-in.
- The web page loads only Planban's own files, its sign-in provider (Clerk) with its sign-in protection, and its live-update service; its security policy refuses anything else. Invitation links are taken out of the address as soon as the page opens.
- **Deleting.** A board you take back to your computer stays online as a read-only copy until you delete it: on the web page, Board settings, Delete online copy. To delete your account, email hello@planban.ai: everything in it is removed within 30 days, including the boards you own.
- Free at launch. Usage limits protect the service: an online board holds up to 256 MB, and each account can make up to 5,000 changes and write up to 64 MB of text a day.

## Updating

Refresh the marketplace and reinstall the plugin so cached skills, hook scripts and MCP instructions update, then start a new session. This release adds dependencies, so updating from v1.1.7 runs a clean dependency installation. Existing local boards keep their storage schema; no migration is needed, and nothing goes online unless you take it there. Projects whose AGENTS.md has a Planban block get its wording updated the next time a board moves online or back to your computer.

From 2.0, Planban on your computer tells the online service which version it is. If an online board uses something your Planban can't show yet, the board page and your agents say so plainly and point you to Update, rather than quietly showing less.
