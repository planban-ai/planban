# Planban v2.0.1

Planban v2.0.1 makes taking a large or older board online more reliable, helps agents keep cards up to date, and lets agents act on what you agree in chat: completing a card, or changing the board's goals.

## Taking a board online

- **Large boards:** if one part of a large upload drops while a board goes online, Planban now tries that part again after a short pause, up to three times, instead of cancelling the whole move.
- **Older boards:** some boards kept older history entries that listed their Specs and Plans as plain file paths, and taking them online stopped with an error. Planban now reads those entries.

## Cards that stay current

- Agents are told plainly that the card is the record: they update it in the same step as a merge, a release, a deploy or passing on your answer, create a card before handing work to another agent, and use Blocked by rather than prose.
- Two new out-of-date warnings: a card that is blocked by a card that is already Complete or Archived, and a card whose next action names a day that has now passed.
- **Needs you** counts In Progress and Up Next cards only, the same on the board and in your list of boards. Before, the list of boards also counted Pending cards.

## Agreeing in chat

- **Completing a card:** when you accept a piece of work in any chat, including one where another agent passes your answer on, or leave the call to the agent, the agent may mark the card Complete. It quotes your words and the day on the card, so the history shows why it closed.
- **Changing goals:** when you agree a change to the board's goals in chat, the agent saves it straight away, with your words and the day, instead of leaving a suggestion for you to accept on the board. Without your agreement it still only suggests, and an agent you've limited to planning on an online board still only suggests. From the command line: `node "$PLANBAN_ROOT/bin/planban.mjs" suggest-goal-change ... --agreed "<your words>"`.

## Updating

Refresh the marketplace and reinstall the plugin so cached skills, hook scripts and MCP instructions update, then start a new session. Dependencies are unchanged from v2.0.0, and existing boards need no migration.
